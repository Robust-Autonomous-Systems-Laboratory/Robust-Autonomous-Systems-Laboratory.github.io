Research

Autonomy in winter weather

How snow, ice, and cold change what autonomous vehicles can sense and how they should drive, from lidar clutter to control on low-friction surfaces.

Lidar perception & metrology

Measuring lidar range error and uncertainty, benchmarking automotive sensors, and making perception work in degraded conditions.

Off-road mobility & planning

Terrain assessment, point-cloud path planning, and multi-robot coordination for ground vehicles in unstructured environments.

Atmospheric & underwater optics

Optical turbulence, imaging through the atmosphere, beam control, and free-space and underwater optical communication.

Open data & software

  • WADS: Winter Adverse Driving DatasetLidar and camera data collected in Upper Peninsula winters
  • WADS-3D3D point clouds of vehicles with snow accumulation
  • ThermalTrackThermal-RGB data for wheel-track detection in snow
  • open-lidar-evaluationROS tools and data for testing automotive lidars in winter weather
  • lidar-uncertainty-modelingPoint-cloud target extraction and range statistics
  • WavePyPython package for wave-optics simulation

Teaching
